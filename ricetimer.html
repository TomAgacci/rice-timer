Rice Timing Engine

Select rice amount, type, and heat level to compute water ratio, hydration load, stir interval, stir count, and exact timing. Total cook time is fixed at 20 minutes: 4 min initial, 3 min final stirring, 13 min absorption.

Inputs

Computed Ratios & Parameters

Rice amount (R)
Water ratio (per cup)
Total water (W)
Hydration load (H)
Heat level (HL)
Stir interval (Δt)
Stir count (Stirs)

Formulas: W = Ratio × R, H = Ratio, Δt = H / HL (minutes), Stirs = floor(3 / Δt) in the 3-minute final stage.

Timing & Stages

Stage Time window Description
Stage 1 — Initial Cook 0:00–4:00 Add rice and water, lid on, bring to simmer at chosen HL. One quick stir at 0:30, then no more stirring until 4:00.
Stage 2 — Final Stirring 4:00–7:00 Stir exactly times, every minutes. Lid off only for brief stirs, then back on.
Stage 3 — Absorption Hold 7:00–20:00 Reduce heat to low (HL ≈ 2–3), lid sealed, no stirring. Rice steams and absorbs water.

Exact Stir Times (Stage 2, 4:00–7:00)

Generated from t_n = 4:00 + n × Δtn = 0 … Stirs − 1.
